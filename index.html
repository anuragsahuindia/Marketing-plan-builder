How it works

The budget split follows fixed rules: each goal weights the channels that serve it, the business model adjusts those weights (B2B leans to LinkedIn, events and sales enablement; local businesses to local search), and channels that can't be funded to a workable monthly minimum are dropped so small budgets stay concentrated. Ten percent is held back for testing and a share goes to tracking, landing pages and creative. Spending limits are calculated only from the numbers you enter. Frameworks come from about 100 concepts across brand, services, digital, B2B, sales and distribution, pricing, product, strategy, consumer behaviour, research, IMC, analytics, growth, CRM, AI and marketing law.

Channel weights and minimum spends are planning rules, not market data. Treat the plan as a starting point and let your first month of results guide the next. Your inputs stay in your browser.
